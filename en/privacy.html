Alarm Pro

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

In short: Alarm Pro doesn’t require an account. Your alarms, groups and sound files are stored only on your devices.

Data stored on your device

  • Alarms, groups, group settings and preferences are stored on your device and are not sent to our servers.
  • Sound files you add are copied into the app on your device to be used as alarm sounds and are not uploaded anywhere.
  • Your Apple Watch receives a summary of your alarms directly from your iPhone.

Permissions

  • Alarms (iPhone): needed so alarms can ring even in Silent mode or with a Focus on.
  • Notifications (Mac): needed to show alarms as notifications on Mac.

Ads

The free version may show a banner ad provided by a third-party ad network (Adsterra), which may process data such as IP address, device information and cookies. Adult and gambling ads are blocked. Subscribers to Premium see no ads.

Subscription and tips

Premium and tips are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. You can cancel the subscription at any time in Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions.

Children

Alarm Pro does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Deleting data

Deleting the app removes all alarms, groups and sounds from your device.
