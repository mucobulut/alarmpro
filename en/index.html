Everything the Clock app is missing

Alarm Pro uses the iPhone's built-in alarm system: alarms ring full screen even in Silent mode or with a Focus on.

A new sound every morning

Something the Clock app can’t do — your alarm plays a different sound each day, all by itself. Add all 74 sounds with one tap, shuffle them, and see the next 7 days in advance — the same sound never plays two days in a row, so you never tune it out.

Collapsible groups

Groups stack neatly and expand with a tap. Every group and every alarm has its own on/off switch.

Group day rules

A weekday group never rings on weekends. Set the days once on the group and they apply to every alarm.

74 original sounds + your own

From vintage phone rings to gentle wake-ups, all made for Alarm Pro. You can also add your own audio files.

Widgets & Control Center

Turn groups and alarms on or off right from the Home Screen, or tap a widget to add and edit alarms.

Apple Watch

See your next alarm, switch groups and alarms, and add or edit alarms from your wrist. Complications included.

Import from Clock

Bring your existing iPhone alarms over in one go with Shortcuts. Duplicates are skipped.

Snooze & Lock Screen

Set snooze per alarm or per group. The countdown appears on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

17 languages, light & dark

Choose from 17 languages right in the app’s settings; light and dark appearance are followed automatically.
